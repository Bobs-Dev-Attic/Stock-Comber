ℹ️ About Stock-Comber

What it is

Stock-Comber is a free, educational value-investing stock screener. It combs publicly traded companies for those that fit the classic criteria of Benjamin Graham (the "defensive investor") and Warren Buffett (durable, high-quality compounders), plus four more value & quality lenses — all from free, public data sources.

It is a research and learning tool, not investment advice. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Data can be delayed, incomplete, or wrong — always verify at the primary source before acting.

How it works

  • Nightly "hidden gems" run. A scheduled job screens a capped, sector-diversified, rotating slice of the market and publishes a ranked shortlist to the dashboard.
  • Custom jobs. Build a reusable screen from your own metric op value rules over a set of tickers or an index template, then run or save it.
  • Manual searches. Look up one ticker for a full deep-dive (all six lenses plus recent news & a sentiment grade), or several to compare in a table.
  • Composite health score. Each company gets a blended 0–100 Value / Quality / Growth score so the shortlist can be ranked at a glance. See Definitions.

Data sources

  • SEC EDGAR (data.sec.gov) — company fundamentals from XBRL financial statements (10-K / 10-Q).
  • Yahoo Finance & Stooq — recent prices and quotes.
  • Finnhub (optional, free tier) — market cap, sector, country and a metrics bundle used to build and enrich the nightly universe.

All are free endpoints. No paid data feeds, and no large-language-model calls — the analysis is transparent, rule-based arithmetic you can audit.
