Did a lens's verdict precede better returns?

Enter a ticker and press Backtest.

For each fiscal year we ask what each value lens would have said using only the fundamentals known then (plus that year-end price), and compare its PASS years vs. FAIL years to the following year's return. SEC fundamentals + Yahoo year-end prices, one name at a time, no dividends or costs — educational, not a track record, not advice.

Which lens had an edge?

“Edge” = average forward return in the lens's PASS years minus its FAIL years. Higher is better; the top row is highlighted.
