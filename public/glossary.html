Valuation

P/E ratio pe_ratio
Price ÷ earnings per share. How many dollars you pay per dollar of annual profit. Lower can mean cheaper — or slower growth.
P/B ratio pb_ratio
Price ÷ book value per share. Below 1.0 means the market values the company under its accounting net worth.
Graham number graham_number
√(22.5 × EPS × book value/share) — Graham's rough ceiling for a defensive purchase price. Price below it suggests a margin of safety.
Earnings yield earnings_yield_pct
Operating earnings ÷ enterprise value, the inverse of a P/E. Higher = cheaper. Half of Greenblatt's Magic Formula.
PEG ratio peg
P/E ÷ earnings growth rate. Near or below 1.0 suggests growth you aren't overpaying for (Lynch's GARP test).
NCAV net_current_asset_value
Net current asset value = current assets − all liabilities. Graham's "net-net" buys below two-thirds of it.

Profitability & quality

ROE % roe_pct
Return on equity = net income ÷ shareholders' equity. How much profit the company earns on each dollar owners have invested.
Return on capital % return_on_capital_pct
Operating profit ÷ (net working capital + net fixed assets). Greenblatt's measure of how productively capital is deployed.
Net margin % net_margin_pct
Net income ÷ revenue — the share of each sales dollar that becomes profit.
Free cash flow free_cash_flow
Operating cash flow − capital expenditures. The cash left over after keeping the business running; funds dividends, buybacks, debt paydown.
Piotroski F-Score
A 0–9 financial-strength score. 8–9 is strong, ≤ 2 is weak. See Strategies.

Financial health

Current ratio current_ratio
Current assets ÷ current liabilities. Above 2.0 is Graham's mark of a strong short-term position.
Debt / equity debt_to_equity
Total (or long-term) debt ÷ equity. Lower means less leverage and less financial risk.
Working capital working_capital
Current assets − current liabilities. A cushion of short-term resources over short-term obligations.
Book value per share book_value_per_share
Shareholders' equity ÷ shares outstanding — the per-share accounting net worth.

Growth & size

Revenue revenue
Total annual sales — a proxy for company size.
EPS eps
Earnings per share = net income ÷ shares outstanding.
Earnings growth 5y % earnings_growth_5y_pct
Cumulative growth in earnings over the last five years.
Revenue growth 5y % revenue_growth_5y_pct
Cumulative growth in revenue over the last five years.
Market cap
Share price × shares outstanding — the market's total price tag on the equity.

Liquidity

Average volume avg_volume
Average daily share volume — how many shares change hands on a typical day. Uses the smoothed 3-month/10-day figure when the analysis is enriched, otherwise the latest day's volume. A rough measure of how actively a stock trades.
$ volume / day dollar_volume
Average daily dollar volume = price × average share volume — the dollars of stock that trade on a typical day. The standard liquidity gauge: higher means you can buy or sell size without moving the price. Screen with e.g. dollar_volume >= 5000000 to skip thinly-traded names.

Stock-Comber scores

Health score 0–100 health_score
A blended composite of Value, Quality, and Growth sub-scores (each 0–100), used to rank the nightly shortlist. Higher is healthier on balance — a summary, not a verdict.
Score %
For a given strategy, the fraction of that checklist's rules the company passed (weighted). Drives "pass / did not pass."
Signal (BUY / WATCH / AVOID)
A plain-language read across the six lenses for recently analyzed tickers. An educational summary of the checklists — not advice.
